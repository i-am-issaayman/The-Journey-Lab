# The Journey Lab

## About this Lab

This is not just code.

This is a system of my journey in tech —

from zero… to whatever “hero” ends up meaning.

A living documentation of:

- the roadmap I follow (and sometimes abandon)
- the projects I build at 2AM
- the bugs that humble me
- the wins that feel too small to celebrate but still matter
- the thoughts I can’t fit into commit messages

It’s part dev log, part diary, part future guide for who I’m becoming.

## The Journey

It hasn’t been clean.

there are gaps in the commits

and sometimes whole branches of doubt.

there are moments of clarity

followed by confusion I can’t debug yet.

it comes with:

- disappointments that teach more than success ever did
- small wins that feel louder than they should
- fear I don’t fully understand, but keep walking through anyway

still… I keep pushing.

because progress doesn’t always look like progress while you’re inside it.
