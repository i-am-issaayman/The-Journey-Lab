Our Story

Founded in 2010, Safari Horizon Tours began with a single Land Cruiser and a passion for Kenya's untouched wilderness. Today, we are a leading eco-tourism provider based in Nairobi.

Our Core Values

  • Conservation: Protecting the habitats we visit.
  • Integrity: Transparent pricing and honest communication.
  • Community: Supporting local Maasai and Samburu communities.
